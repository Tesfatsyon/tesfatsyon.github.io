About

I am a PhD candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Western University, supervised by Prof. Girma Bitsuamlak and Prof. Katarina Grolinger. My background is in mechanical and thermal engineering, with an MSc from Warsaw University of Technology.

My research covers building energy modelling, climate-adaptive design optimization, and machine-learning surrogate models interpreted with explainable AI. I am extending this work to energy-efficient cooling for data centres.

Tools: Python (machine learning), C/C++, MATLAB, EnergyPlus, DesignBuilder, IES VE, Revit, AutoCAD, SketchUp

Research

  • Climate-adaptive robust design optimization
  • Climate-guided surrogate models with explainable AI
  • AI for energy-efficient data centre cooling
