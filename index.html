Tesfatsyon Simon Ochono

PhD Candidate·Civil and Environmental Engineering·Western University, London, Ontario, Canada

Tesfatsyon Simon Ochono is a PhD can­di­date in Civil and En­vi­ron­men­tal En­gi­neer­ing (Smart Cities) at West­ern Uni­ver­sity, sup­erv­ised by Prof. Girma Bitsuamlak and Prof. Katarina Grolinger. Tesfatsyon's re­search com­bines phys­ics-based sim­u­la­tion and data-driven ap­proaches for en­ergy-ef­fi­cient, com­fort­able and low-car­bon build­ing de­signs as the cli­mate changes.

Tesfatsyon's re­search also in­volves de­sign of sus­tai­nable data cen­ters, whose en­ergy de­mand is ris­ing quickly with the growth of AI. Tesfatsyon earned a BSc in Me­chan­i­cal En­gi­neer­ing from Arba Minch Uni­ver­sity and an MSc in Power En­gi­neer­ing (Ther­mal) from Warsaw Uni­ver­sity of Tech­nol­ogy, and now serves as a grad­u­ate teach­ing as­sis­tant at West­ern in courses on HVAC, build­ing sus­tain­abil­ity and com­pu­ta­tional tools for engineers.
